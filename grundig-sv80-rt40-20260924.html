5. 音質如何？（重點）
5. How does it sound? (the key section)

5.1 當年定位：1965 年嘅「大力靚機」
5.1 Its position in 1965: high power, high quality

1965 年德國 HiFi 標準（DIN 45500）只要求連續輸出 2 × 7 W；SV 80 做到 2 × 30 W 連續，屬超額規格。德國 HiFi 博物館評語：「SV 80 喺佢嘅年代係一件傑出產品，普通人亦負擔得起」。有德國用家帖文就講：「SV 80 在 1966 年係最強嘅立體聲擴音機」。

The German HiFi standard of 1965 (DIN 45500) required only 2 × 7 W continuous; the SV 80 delivers 2 × 30 W continuous — over-specified for its day. The German HiFi Museum calls it "an outstanding product of its time, one that even ordinary buyers could afford", and a German forum post notes that in 1966 the SV 80 was the most powerful stereo amp Grundig made.

5.2 技術上點解會有種「暖厚圓潤」聲
5.2 Why it sounds warm, fat and round

  • 鍺（germanium）功率管：輸出級每聲道 4 粒 AD166 鍺管。鍺管開關速度慢、內阻大、失真天生比矽管高 —— 呢個正是「暖、厚、圓潤」嘅來源；同時前級用低噪矽管（BSY76／BC107），保留細節。
    Germanium output devices: four AD166 germanium power transistors per channel. Germanium is slower, has higher internal resistance and inherently higher distortion than silicon — the classic recipe for a warm, fat, rounded sound, while the preamp uses low-noise silicon (BSY76/BC107) to keep detail.
  • 直接耦合、無輸出變壓器、無輸出電容：1965 年屬先進設計（同代真空管機仍用變壓器），低頻損耗少、速度較好。
    Direct-coupled, no output transformer and no output capacitor: advanced for 1965 (tube amps of the era still used transformers), giving lower bass loss and better speed.
  • 阻尼係數只有 20（現代機普遍 > 100）：擴音機對喇叭嘅「煞車力」較弱 → 低頻量感多、收放較鬆，配硬推嘅喇叭容易「boom」。呢點解釋咗下面用家嘅配搭忠告。
    Damping factor of only 20 (modern amps are usually > 100): weaker control over the speaker — plenty of bass weight but looser, which is why hard-to-drive speakers can boom.
  • 失真 0.5%、頻率響應 ±1 dB（20 Hz–20 kHz）：以今日標準屬偏高，但 1965 年係水準以上；功率頻寬去到 50 kHz（1% THD）反映高頻設計唔錯。
    0.5% THD, ±1 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz: high by modern standards, good for 1965; the 50 kHz power bandwidth (at 1% THD) shows decent high-frequency design.

5.4 優點 vs 弱點
5.4 Strengths vs weaknesses

評價Assessment
👍 優點：厚暖圓潤音色、低頻量感足，聽人聲／爵士／古典舒服；唱頭放大質素高（黑膠友好）；用料同耐用度有口碑（39 年電容仍合格）；維修設計貼心（掀板式底盤、可換保險絲）；1960 年代德國工業設計代表作，外觀有味道。👍 Strengths: warm, fat, rounded tone with generous bass — lovely for vocals, jazz and classical; genuinely good phono stage (vinyl-friendly); well-regarded build and longevity (caps fine after 39 years); service-friendly design (hinged chassis, plug-in fuses); an icon of 1960s German industrial design.
👎 弱點：阻尼係數低 → 難推喇叭會 boom；分析力、動態、寧靜度不及現代機；失真 0.5%（現代標準偏高）；原版亞克力面板易刮花；部分高電平輸入噪聲較大；30 W 唔適合大空間／大食喇叭。👎 Weaknesses: low damping factor — boomy with hard-to-drive speakers; resolution, dynamics and quietness are below modern standards; 0.5% THD is high today; the original acrylic front scratches easily; some line inputs are noisy; 30 W is not for large rooms or power-hungry speakers.

5.5 點配搭最好聽（實用建議）
5.5 How to get the best out of it

  • 喇叭：4–16Ω、靈敏度 ≥ 89–90 dB、阻抗平順嘅書架或中型座地；當年配 Grundig 自家 Box 系列最穩。
    Speakers: 4–16Ω, sensitivity ≥ 89–90 dB, benign impedance — bookshelf or medium floorstander; period Grundig Box models are a safe match.
  • 訊源：黑膠（MM 唱頭）＋卡座／調諧器最夾；接現代 DAC／CD（輸出 2 V）對 1965 年線路偏大，如有失真可加衰減或用其他輸入試。
    Sources: vinyl (MM cartridge) with cassette deck or tuner suits it best; a modern DAC/CD at 2 V is hot for 1965 circuitry — if it distorts, use an attenuator or try another input.
  • 空間：30 W 適合細至中型房間；想爆棚要換現代大功率機。
    Room: 30 W suits small to medium rooms; for loud, large-scale listening you need a modern high-power amp.
  • 保養：接電前確認電壓選擇在 220 V（香港）；建議搵技師檢查電源電容、偏壓、旋鈕清潔，之後先長期使用。
    Maintenance: make sure the voltage selector is set to 220 V (Hong Kong); have a technician check the power capacitors, bias and switch/pot cleaning before long-term use.

6. RT 40 調諧器：音質如何？
6. RT 40 tuner: how does it sound?

1965 年 RT 40 係全晶體管設計（27 粒晶體管，前身 RT 50 仍用真空管），內建立體聲解碼器、貓眼調諧指示、可關 AFC —— 當年係先進水平。聲音取向同 SV 80 一致：模擬調諧器嘅自然、厚暖。有位實測者建議：「如果你有訊號良好嘅古典音樂電台，用呢部機配一部老式模擬調諧器，你會愛上嗰種自然同有生命力嘅聲音」。

In 1965 the RT 40 was a fully transistorised design (27 transistors; its predecessor RT 50 was valve-based), with a built-in stereo decoder, a "magic eye" tuning indicator and switchable AFC — advanced for the day. Its character matches the SV 80: natural, warm, analogue tuner sound. One reviewer advises: "if you have a well-received classical radio station, try this amp with a vintage analogue tuner — you will love how natural and alive it sounds".

評價Assessment
👍 優點：聲音自然厚暖、耐聽；FM 靈敏度 1.4 µV 以當年屬好；貓眼指示同機械手感有味道；中／長波鐵氧體天線方便。👍 Strengths: natural, warm, easy to listen to; 1.4 µV FM sensitivity was good for the era; the magic-eye indicator and mechanical feel are delightful; ferrite rod for MW/LW is handy.
👎 弱點：頻率響應只到 12.5 kHz、立體聲分離度 35 dB、失真 0.5% → 高頻延伸同立體感不及現代；今日香港 MW／LW 已無本地廣播，只剩 FM 可用；60 年老機 IF／中周唔好亂調（需儀器），AFC 開關接點易氧化。👎 Weaknesses: response stops at 12.5 kHz, 35 dB stereo separation, 0.5% distortion — less top-end and stereo image than a modern tuner; MW/LW have no local broadcast left in Hong Kong (FM only); do not touch the IF transformers on a 60-year-old set without instruments, and AFC switch contacts oxidise.

The data above suggests this is more likely a level/impedance match issue (a 2 V CD output is hot for 1965 circuitry, and a damping factor of 20 means looser bass control) rather than the CD format itself. If you want to run modern sources into the SV 80, try an attenuator, or use the TUNER/TB line inputs and adjust gradually.

10. Summary in one breath

你手上呢套係 1965 年西德 Grundig HiFi-Studio-Serie 嘅旗艦組合（SV 80 擴音機 ＋ RT 40 調諧器，非 M 金屬面板版）。佢嘅聲音係典型早期鍺晶體管味道：厚、暖、圓潤、低頻有量感，唱頭放大質素出名好，配黑膠同卡座最靚；代價係阻尼係數低、分析力同失真數字追唔上現代機，要配靈敏易推喇叭。只要電容同偏壓檢查過，呢套 60 年機今日仍然聽得過癮 —— 佢係「韻味」型，唔係「分析」型。

You have the flagship pair of Grundig's 1965 HiFi-Studio-Serie (SV 80 amplifier + RT 40 tuner, the non-M panels). The sound is classic early germanium: thick, warm, round, with generous bass; its phono stage is famous for its quality and it shines with vinyl and cassette. The price is a low damping factor and measured distortion that cannot match modern gear, so pair it with sensitive, easy-to-drive speakers. Once the capacitors and bias are checked, this 60-year-old set is still a pleasure to listen to — it is a "character" amplifier, not an "analytical" one.
